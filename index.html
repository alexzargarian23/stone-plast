Built for real installations

PVC marble sheeting solves problems natural stone can’t — without giving up the finish people actually want.

Roughly a quarter lighter

At an average density of 1,985.9 kg/m³, Stone Plast sheeting is markedly lighter than natural marble slab, easing handling, framing loads and shipping.

Moisture-resistant PVC core

Unlike porous natural stone, the PVC base doesn’t need sealing against everyday moisture — suited to kitchens, bathrooms and wet areas.

Full original-size sheets

Panels are produced at full sheet size rather than small tiles, giving fewer seams and faster coverage across walls and surfaces.

One supplier, one showroom

Marble and stone patterns, solid studio colors, and matching accessories are manufactured and sourced through a single Tehran-based facility.
