Tested, and paperwork to prove it

Every Stone Plast sheet is produced to the same specification that went through independent testing. Expand either card for the full lab summary.

Reaction-to-fire classified under EN 13501‑1 and Iranian National Standard 8299‑1 by the Road, Housing & Urban Development Research Center. Classification covers flame spread, heat release and smoke production (s2) with no flaming droplets (d0) — the profile specified for wall linings in occupied commercial interiors.

Pasteur Institute of Iran testing against E. coli (ATCC 25922) recorded over 99.9% bacterial reduction after 24 hours of surface contact, using the same sheet finish sold across the collection — not a treated test sample.
